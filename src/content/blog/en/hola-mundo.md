Welcome! This is the first post on my personal website. If you're reading this, the blog works. 🎉

## Why Astro

I wanted a **fast** website that's easy to maintain and focused on content. Astro generates static
HTML by default, so pages load instantly and SEO is excellent with no effort.
I write the projects and posts in Markdown and the site rebuilds itself.

## What you'll find here

- **Projects** — things I've built, with the technical context behind each one.
- **Blog** — notes on what I'm learning along the way.
- **About** — my background and how to get in touch.
